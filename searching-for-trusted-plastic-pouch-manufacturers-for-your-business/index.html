Why Plastic Pouches are Going Mainstream

Many of the classic packaging styles of glass, tin cans, and rigid plastic boxes are being supplanted by plastic pouches. Here is why companies and consumers favor them:

  • Lightweight & Portable – Portable storage and convenience.
  • Cost-Effective – Saves on packaging expenses.
  • Reusable Design – Comes in various shapes, sizes and finishes.
  • Durability – High oxygen, moisture and contamination barrier properties.
  • Eco-Friendly Features – Zippers, resealable lids and spout make it reusable.
  • Eco-Friendly Alternatives – Some options are biodegradable.

This functional and aesthetic pairing has given the plastic pouch one of the world's most quickly growing packaging solutions.
