WHO ARE WE?

Packaging That Protects, Quality That Performs.

Rajdhani Packaging is a leading plastic pouch manufacturer in Delhi NCR, based in Khera Kalan Industrial Area, New Delhi. We make durable, high-quality pouches and bags for food, snacks, spices, seeds, flour, rice, tea and detergent powder brands. Every product is designed to protect what's inside and help your brand look its best on the shelf.

As a direct manufacturer, we offer custom sizes, materials and printing to match your exact requirements. Whether you need stand-up pouches, zipper pouches, three-side seal pouches or spout pouches, our team helps you choose the right packaging for your product and budget.
