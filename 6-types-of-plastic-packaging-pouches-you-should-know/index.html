Why Choose Plastic Packaging Pouches?

Plastic packaging pouches have become the preferred choice over traditional rigid packaging. Here's why businesses are switching:

  • Lightweight & Handy – Saves shipping expenses.
  • Reusable Design – Different styles, amounts, and finishes in store.
  • High Durability – Protects against moisture, oxygen, and leaks.
  • User-Friendly Features – Zippers, spouts, and seals.
  • Green Choices – Recyclable and eco-friendly products offered.

These advantages suit them best in industries involving foodstuffs, beverages, cosmetics, drugs, farming, and many more.

Six Types of Plastic Packaging Pouches:

At Rajdhani Packaging, we execute a vast array of custom-designed plastic packaging pouches to order:

  1. Stand-Up Pouch

    Best for snacks, dry fruits, pulses, and spices. These remain standing upright on shelves and give a better appearance.

  2. Zip & Resealable Pouch

    Ideal for products that are multifunctional like powders, coffee, and animal food.

  3. Printed Pouches

    Premium printing with artwork, logo, and colors to aid in brand awareness.

  4. Spout Pouch

    Ideal for fluids such as oils, sauces, and juices — travel friendly and leak tight.

  5. Vacuum & Barrier Pouches

    Maintains freshness by preventing air and moisture penetration. Ideal for perishable items.

  6. Flat Bottom & Gusseted Pouches

    Provide a stable base and clarity storage capacity, best suited to bulky goods.
